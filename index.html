Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is Exclusive vs Inclusive GST?

Exclusive GST means tax is added on top of the net product price. Inclusive GST means the total invoice amount already contains the tax component.

Q2: Is my calculation data private?

Yes, 100%. All computations run locally inside your device browser using JavaScript without transmitting any financial numbers to external servers.
